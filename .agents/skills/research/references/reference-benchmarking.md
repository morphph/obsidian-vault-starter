# Reference and golden-example benchmarking

Use this when the user provides a report, article, deck, or other “golden example” and asks
research to meet or exceed its standard.

## Evidence boundary

The reference is a benchmark for reader value, structure, coverage, and actionability. It is not
evidence unless its underlying sources are independently opened and verified. Never preserve a
claim merely because it makes the reference persuasive.

Record in `research-plan.md`:

- what the reference does well and should be preserved;
- its load-bearing facts, exact numbers, rankings, quotes, identities, commands, and advice to
  audit;
- the reader and decision the optimized report should serve;
- a fixed comparison rubric before drafting.

## Comparison rubric

Score the reference, first run, and optimized run on the same 0–10 dimensions:

1. reader value and answer-first clarity;
2. scanability and cognitive sequence;
3. concrete actionability;
4. source quality and source-role diversity;
5. claim-level traceability;
6. community-channel richness without false ranking;
7. counter-evidence, uncertainty, and risk boundaries;
8. freshness and technical-contract accuracy.

Do not optimize for total length or source count. A shorter report can win by making decisions
clearer and claims more auditable.

## Preserve, correct, add

Build the comparison in three layers:

- **Preserve:** strong reader journey, useful tables, commands, scenarios, examples, and cost
  explanations.
- **Correct:** unsupported superlatives, stale facts, misread metrics, hidden affiliations,
  repeated evidence chains, mixed product surfaces, and recommendations that outrun evidence.
- **Add:** explicit technical contracts, counter-evidence, reversible experiments, risk Gates,
  and what evidence would change the recommendation.

When the user asks for iterative optimization, write the initial four artifacts before changing
the skill or process. Then apply only generalizable improvements, rerun into the next available
`-rerun-N` workspace, and write a concise comparison artifact. Keep topic-specific conclusions
out of the reusable skill.

## Quality threshold

An optimized report reaches the reference standard only when it:

- preserves the reference's reader value and practical specificity;
- improves every known factual or attribution error rather than repeating it;
- scores at least as high overall on the fixed rubric;
- has no unresolved high-severity claim-to-ledger or recommendation-risk failure;
- states remaining access limits and unresolved questions instead of hiding them.

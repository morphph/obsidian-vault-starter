# Editorial review

Revise in passes. Do not only proofread.

## 1. Contract pass

- Is the intended reader obvious?
- Does the article deliver the topic brief's promise?
- Is the selected thesis clear within the first 20%?
- Did rejected angles leak back into the article?

## 2. Argument pass

- Does each section advance the thesis?
- Are causal steps explained rather than asserted?
- Is the strongest reasonable objection represented fairly?
- Does the conclusion follow from the evidence?
- Can background be shortened without losing understanding?

## 3. Evidence pass

- Can every load-bearing claim reach an original source?
- Are time-sensitive claims still current?
- Are popularity and superlative claims justified?
- Are inference and author judgment presented as such?
- Are quotations accurate and necessary?

## 4. Reader pass

- Are technical terms explained at first use?
- Does the article assume private vault context?
- Are examples concrete enough to transfer?
- Are headings useful without turning the article into notes?
- Does the ending leave a useful decision, lens, or action?

## 5. Prose pass

Remove:

- generic AI-era openings;
- repeated conclusions;
- throat-clearing and research-process narration;
- stacked abstractions;
- filler transitions such as `值得注意的是` when nothing surprising follows;
- unnecessary English where clear Chinese exists;
- placeholders, TODOs, and internal instructions.

Read difficult paragraphs aloud. Split sentences that require rereading, but do not flatten all
rhythm into uniformly short prose.

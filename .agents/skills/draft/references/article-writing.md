# Article writing

Choose a structure that serves the thesis. Do not apply every pattern.

## Analytical article

```text
Concrete tension or consequence
→ thesis
→ necessary model or distinction
→ evidence and examples
→ strongest objection
→ implications
→ changed understanding or action
```

## Explanatory article

```text
Why the reader is confused
→ simplest accurate definition
→ mechanism
→ comparison with adjacent concepts
→ real example
→ limits and misuse
→ practical takeaway
```

## Guide

```text
Outcome and prerequisites
→ mental model
→ staged procedure
→ worked example
→ failure modes
→ decision checklist
```

## Opening discipline

A strong opening does at least two of these:

- names a consequence the reader recognizes;
- creates a precise unanswered question;
- challenges a common but consequential assumption;
- shows a concrete scene, result, or contrast;
- promises a useful distinction.

Avoid generic openings such as `AI is changing rapidly`, dictionary definitions without stakes,
and long histories before the reader knows why they matter.

## Section discipline

For every section, be able to complete:

> This section changes the reader's understanding by ______, which the thesis needs because ______.

If the sentence is weak, merge, move, or delete the section.

## Explanation

- Define a term once, then use it consistently.
- Compare concepts along stable dimensions.
- Prefer one developed example over several named examples.
- State what an analogy cannot explain.
- Repeat the central distinction in different contexts, not the same wording.

## Endings

Do not merely summarize headings. Resolve the opening tension and leave the reader with a decision,
new lens, or small action proportionate to the evidence.

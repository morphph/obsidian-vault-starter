# Feitong Yang

## Summary
Founding engineer at Shortcut.ai (Excel AI coworker) and former AI agent startup builder. Cognitive scientist by training, writing at feitong.phd. Author of "The Ten Commandments for Building Product" — a first-hand account of three pivots and what product-market fit actually cost.

## Details
- **Background:** Engineer + cognitive scientist (PhD). Builds AI agents and writes about how minds and machines reason, remember, and build
- **Company arc:** Two years at one AI agent startup; three products across three pivots:
  1. **Minecraft coplayer** — multi-agent building demo (300+ items); viral on HN, but "never clear the attention reflected a need anyone had"
  2. **Fairies** — general-purpose desktop agent; blank-chat-box affordance problem killed it
  3. **Shortcut** — Excel AI coworker (shortcut.ai); found PMF in finance/high-caliber Excel users
- **Vantage point:** "Founding engineer... front-row seat to nearly every decision" — this is operator intel, not VC/researcher speculation
- **Writing style:** Precise, unsentimental. Calls out specific failures with names and numbers. Cites Sam Altman's "atrocious retention" directly

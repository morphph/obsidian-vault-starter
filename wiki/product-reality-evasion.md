# Product Reality Evasion

## Summary
[[feitong-yang]]'s name for the "one disease underneath most" zero-to-one failures: any move that promises to spare you painful, first-hand contact with reality while feeling like progress. AI dramatically lowers the cost of building, making evasion easier and more dangerous than ever.

## Details

**The core pattern:** Every evasion "feels like moving forward" but is "a way of standing still while feeling busy":
- Reading logs instead of watching a user
- Having someone "run user research and bring you findings" while you keep decision authority
- Awarding yourself the certainty of a great founder
- Building the "correct" architecture before finding a single customer
- Using vibe coding to ship 10K lines without market contact

**Why AI makes it worse, not better:**
> "AI lowers the cost of building so far that the old ways of avoiding reality now look like extraordinary productivity."

Feitong's specific new evasions AI enables:
1. **Fake scale:** vibe coding makes "ship for every user" feel free — you avoid choosing your user (Commandment 3)
2. **Fake architecture:** coding agents let you build million-user infra on day 0 — you avoid discovering what the product actually is (Commandment 8)
3. **Fake product:** agent demos a breakthrough in an afternoon without ever meeting a user (Commandment 4)
4. **Fake progress:** LOC count → Garry Tan's "37K lines/day" boast (Commandment 7)

**The hard part AI didn't automate:**
> "When building gets this cheap, building stops being the thing that separates you; judgment does — knowing who you are for, what they need, and whether they will come back and pay. AI automated the part that was never the hard part. The hard part is still yours."

**Canonical test for any action:** Is this creating first-hand contact with the market's verdict, or avoiding it?

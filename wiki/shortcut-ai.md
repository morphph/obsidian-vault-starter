# Shortcut

## Summary
Excel AI coworker (shortcut.ai) — the third and successful pivot by [[feitong-yang]]'s team. Found PMF by narrowing from "all Excel users" to finance professionals doing high-caliber work. The clearest affordance case study in his Ten Commandments essay.

## Details
- **What it does:** AI agent embedded beside a spreadsheet; automates Excel work with an agent panel visible on day one
- **Affordance:** "Looked like a spreadsheet with an agent panel beside it" — every user understood at a glance without explanation
- **PMF journey:** Started with "all Excel users," progressively narrowed to finance/high-caliber work segment; casual users ("opens Excel to jot numbers") never converted
- **Key UX lesson:** Affordance came from the form itself. The shape told the story; no onboarding copy needed
- **Retention pattern:** Launch video caused DAU spike, then rapid drop. Long-term climbing came from product quality alone — classic "attention is spark, product is fuel" story
- **Claim:** "We think Shortcut is the best spreadsheet agent on the market" — team's stated belief as of Jun 2026
